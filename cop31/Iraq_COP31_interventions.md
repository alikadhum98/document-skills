# مداخلات العراق في COP31 كما ثبتت في تسجيلات Krisp

طوبقت يوم 26/11/2026 مع تسجيلات Krisp ومع ما أعطاه المستخدم. النص الإنكليزي للأولى والثالثة معاد بناؤه من التفريغ الآلي ومن المسودة المعدة قبل الإلقاء، فهو قريب من الملقى لا حرفي. والثانية والرابعة طابق المستخدم نصهما.

## ١. تنفيذ إرشادات المادة 6.2 والمراجعة الفنية، 14/11

الجلسة: المشاورات غير الرسمية لبند CMA 16(a). التسجيل: Krisp 01a1d547a6da77898b0736f632019052 (عنوانه Mobile recording)، نحو الدقيقة 38:40. وأيدتها المجموعة العربية بالاسم، وأشارت إليها LMDC.

```
Thank you, Co-facilitators. Iraq aligns with the Arab Group and the LMDC. We agree with Japan that this is a phase of learning by doing, in which Parties apply the guidance in line with their national circumstances.

In response to your call for guidance to the Secretariat, we see three points. First, as paragraph 64 of the report shows, Parties struggled to understand how significant inconsistencies are determined and how the different reports fit together. We ask the Secretariat to prioritize capacity-building on these points, including through the regional collaboration centres, and to direct the remaining resources mentioned in paragraph 78 to host countries preparing their first reports.

Second, reviews should remain facilitative, non-intrusive and non-punitive, as called for in paragraph 41, and additional information provided during reviews should be reflected in the main report.

Third, clarity should come through the reviewer manual and practical examples, not new requirements before the 2028 review. Thank you.
```

رقم الفقرة 78 من المسودة، والتفريغ الآلي سمعه (817).

## ٢. تمويل بنية المادة 6.2، CMA 18، 16/11

الجلسة: المشاورات غير الرسمية لبند CMA 18. التسجيل: Krisp 01a1db871c71771b8a90e6c53160c0f5، نحو الدقيقة 22:31. وخلاصتها التي أرسلها المستخدم في الواتساب: (أن يشمل الإعفاء فتح قسم البلد في السجل الدولي وتشغيله معا، ورفض ربط عدم دفع الرسوم باتساق نتائج التخفيف، لأن فحص الاتساق يتعلق بالسلامة البيئية لا بالالتزام المالي).

```
Thank you, Co-facilitators. Iraq aligns with the statements of the Arab Group and the LMDC, and we will not repeat them.

We would add two points. First, as a host country preparing to participate, we see the exemption for developing countries as covering both the opening and the operation of a Party's section in the international registry, since the registry exists to enable participation.

Second, non-payment of a fee should never be linked to the consistency of mitigation outcomes. Consistency checks concern environmental integrity, not financial compliance. We therefore cannot accept paragraph 14. Thank you.
```

## ٣. مراجعة برنامج عمل النهج غير السوقية، المادة 6.8، 17/11

الجلسة: المشاورات غير الرسمية لبند CMA 16(c) صباحا. التسجيل: Krisp 01a1e577f83f7148925788719a046aa5، نحو الدقيقة 07:24، وتلتها مداخلة قصيرة ثانية عند 10:41 في القسم الأول من النص (ربط أولويات المساهمات بالدعم، وحذف فقرة المستودع، وتوسيع الاستعمال ومعالجة العوائق، ودعم المنصة لتطوير النهج).

```
Thank you, Co-facilitators. Iraq aligns itself with the LMDC. We cannot support closing the work programme under Article 6, paragraph 8. As the LMDC said, non-market approaches are at the heart of cooperation under Article 6 and part of its balance. This review has shown that the work is unfinished, not that it is done.

We invite colleagues who see closure as an option to consider what that would mean for Parties that are only now starting to use this framework.

We are ready to work towards a reasonable middle ground here, but if the choice is between closing the work programme and continuing our discussions next year, Iraq would prefer to continue. Thank you.
```

## ٤. الحوار الوزاري لتحدي إدارة الكربون، 19/11

التسجيل: Krisp 01a1ea568e25732f900c4263787e6f23. وهي مشاركة في حوار رئاسي، لا مداخلة في مفاوضات المادة السادسة.

```
Thank you, Chair. Iraq thanks the COP31 Presidency for convening this discussion.

As an oil and gas producing developing country, Iraq sees carbon management as one of several options that can support emission reductions in our energy and industrial sectors, alongside our ongoing efforts to reduce gas flaring and improve energy efficiency.

How far and how fast such technologies can be deployed depends on national circumstances, including geological storage assessment, regulatory frameworks, infrastructure, and access to finance and technology.

In this regard, international cooperation in technical assistance, capacity-building and knowledge sharing would be valuable.

Iraq will continue to follow these discussions with interest. Thank you.
```
